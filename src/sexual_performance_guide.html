Squeeze the muscle you'd use to stop urine flow. Hold 2–3 seconds, release. Supports erection rigidity and prevents blood from leaving the penis prematurely.

Daily30 min

Moderate pace, every day. Clinically linked to a 41% reduction in ED risk — and has been shown to fully restore performance in overweight men.
